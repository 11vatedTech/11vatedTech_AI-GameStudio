What this proves: the local art pipeline can produce a coherent, painterly environment, bespoke torch, readable combatants, and expressive purple smoke. What it does not prove: animation, combat feel, engine integration, or browser performance.

Side-on cinematic

Generated · seed 7183401

Strong character and torch silhouette, dramatic face-to-face staging, and a sculptural smoke ribbon. Reads like a cinematic action keyframe.

Trade-off: the cloud looks beautiful, but its gameplay footprint and route occupancy are less explicit.

Elevated three-quarter

Generated · seed 7183402

Shows the ground plane, cloud coverage, surrounding path, and enemy position. Better communicates where a tactical smoke ability controls space.

Trade-off: the hero is smaller and the staging is less emotionally close; play-scale silhouette must be tested in-engine.

Early read—not a final camera decision

The elevated study is currently more informative for this dominion’s zone-control verb; the side-on study is more immediately cinematic. Keep both candidates until the same hero/torch/rogue are integrated into a real Godot scene and tested with a readable attack tell, movement, and the haze effect at target scale.

Art quality bar

  • Restrained slate/teal ruins with layered painterly materials.
  • Amethyst smoke has a designed silhouette and luminous core.
  • Dedicated lantern head reads as a crafted weapon.
  • Next: inspect small-scale readability, smoke opacity, identity consistency, and animation in time.

Blender motion test · camera-only push

This is a real 24-frame Blender render sequence at 8 fps, assembled into a loop. It gently pushes toward the smoke and returns. The characters, torch, and smoke are one still image card: this proves camera/scene motion only, not character animation, moving smoke, or gameplay. Next gate is authored or rigged Torch character action integrated at actual game scale.
