# The master plan: an original, AI-produced fantasy action RPG

We're going to approach this as an 11vatedTech game production—not a quick prototype dressed up with effects. The goal is a visually coherent, combat-rich, explorable vertical slice that proves the larger open-world RPG is worth building.

I've checked the current [AI Browser Game Jam 5 rules](https://itch.io/jam/ai-jam-5?utm_source=chatgpt.com) and your [11vatedTech AI Game Studio repository](https://github.com/11vatedTech/11vatedTech_AI-GameStudio?utm_source=chatgpt.com). The jam requires a free game that runs in the browser, substantial AI-assisted creation, and a majority of the submission's content to be new if continuing an existing project. Its categories include graphics, audio, fun, theme, overall, and AI usage. The published deadline is October 31 at 03:00 UTC, equivalent to October 30 at 11:00 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time. We'll plan to finish earlier, not gamble on the final hour.&#x20;

Your studio already provides a relevant foundation: local image-generation adapters, asset processing, animation diagnostics, provenance tracking, Godot support, and release checks. Its own documentation also acknowledges the critical distinction we need to respect: generating an image, producing a valid asset file, integrating an asset into the game, and verifying that it looks good in actual gameplay are four different milestones.

That distinction will define our entire production process.

The visual-quality rule: we will not use geometric primitives as substitutes for characters, environments, props, creatures, or effects that ought to be authored visual assets. We will generate, select, refine, animate, integrate, and inspect those assets. Code will still handle gameplay, collision, lighting logic, shaders, and responsive effects where appropriate; those systems will support the artwork rather than serve as a replacement for it.

## The GDD is ready for our first review

Download the Torch RPG GDD — v0.1 (Markdown)

The document is approximately 7,600 words and establishes our initial production baseline. It includes:

- The complete creative vision, design pillars, and distinction between the full open-world game and the jam vertical slice.
- All ten flame dominions, including their combat identities, exploration roles, and design requirements.
- The three-question identity ritual and the proposed affinity system for starting torches.
- Physical torch combat, signature abilities, combos, enemy behaviors, and the flame-eater faction.
- The opening region, story progression, encounter sequence, and climactic transformation.
- The visual art bible and a detailed AI-generated asset, character-consistency, animation, VFX, UI, and audio workflow.
- Technical architecture, accessibility, risk management, production milestones, acceptance gates, and evidence requirements.

### The most important production decision

We are not going to mass-generate assets and hope they look good together.

First, we will establish the art direction and produce a small, representative proof set: the protagonist, a torch, an enemy, an environment, the signature flames, and a meaningful animation. We'll examine them together, in the intended camera and at actual gameplay scale. If the workflow cannot maintain visual identity and animation quality, we fix the workflow before generating the rest of the game.

The GDD deliberately keeps the camera choice open until that test. My recommended starting direction is painterly mythic dark fantasy with anime-scale combat spectacle, but the actual game needs to prove that its characters, world, animation, and effects look coherent together.

Our next milestone is the visual and animation proof—not another round of abstract planning. Once that passes, the GDD can be refined into an implementation-ready production contract for the 11vatedTech AI Game Studio, with every essential asset accounted for and every major feature tied to an acceptance test.
